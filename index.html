What is Moodi?

Moodi was built for the fans who feel music differently. Whether you're windows-down on a night drive, deep in your feelings at 2am, or just need something to soundtrack your chaos — Moodi cuts through the noise and finds the emo, pop punk, rock and metal tracks that match exactly where you're at.

Most music apps drown you in endless queues and algorithmically bloated playlists. Moodi does the opposite. Answer a few quick questions about your mood and moment, and we hand you a tight list — no bigger than a regular album — so you can just hit play and feel it on your Spotify account.

No rabbit holes. No decision paralysis. Just twelve songs that get it.
